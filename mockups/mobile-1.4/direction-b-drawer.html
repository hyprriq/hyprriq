Direction B — Hamburger drawer (“same desk, smaller desk”)

The conservative move: the desktop sidebar becomes a slide-in drawer, structure untouched — same groups, same order, same credits widget. The cost is honesty about taps: every navigation is now two interactions (open, choose), and the hamburger lives at the top — the hardest place to reach one-handed. Content patterns (attention card, KPI 2×2, case cards) are shared with Direction A; only the chrome differs.
