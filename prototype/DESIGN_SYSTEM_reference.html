02 · Typography

Fraunces (serif) + Instrument Sans + JetBrains Mono

A characterful serif for headings gives the research-firm gravitas; a clean humanist sans carries the body; mono handles IDs and data. Explicitly not Inter — the serif is the personality that separates this from a generic dashboard.

03 · Verdict system — the signature element

Four ranked levels, one consistent encoding

The four verdicts are the product. They read as a ranked spectrum (best → worst), never a crude traffic light. Colour + a left-border rank marker + position on the spectrum all reinforce order. This encoding appears identically everywhere a verdict shows — dashboard chip, report headline, spectrum.

Source Clear
Level 1 of 4 · strongest
Supplier, brand environment, and documents all consistent with credible sourcing. No significant gaps.
Usable With Conditions
Level 2 of 4
Credible, but specific items need verification. A checklist, not a red flag.
Verify Before Purchase
Level 3 of 4
Important evidence missing or unclear. Do not proceed past a test order until items are confirmed.
Do Not Rely
Level 4 of 4 · weakest
Serious gaps or warning signals. Cannot support relying on this source with available evidence.

04 · Confidence & the honesty treatment

Uncertainty shown as integrity, never failure

Two confidence states only. And the confirmed / could-not-confirm split is a trust feature — designed as honesty, never as an accusation against the supplier. "Not assessed" is a calm absence, not a finding.

● Verified ○ Inferred Not assessed Informational — does not affect the verdict

Confirmed

  • Established, independently verifiable wholesale distributor.
  • Long, continuous trading and registration record.
  • No negative reputation, fraud, or warning signals.

Could not confirm

  • An independent authorization link to your account for the brands in scope.
  • That the specific goods originate through an authorized channel for resale.

Design note: on a Source Clear case the right column may be nearly empty — the layout must stay balanced, not look broken.
