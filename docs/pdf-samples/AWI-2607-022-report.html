The single most important risk

The operative risk is that a buyer purchasing Bosch products through TD SYNNEX for resale on Amazon in the US market may lack the Bosch-issued authorization required to list and sell without facing listing removal, account suspension, or legal action from Bosch — outcomes Bosch's own documented enforcement posture makes plausible. This risk is resolvable before commitment: TD SYNNEX can either produce a current Bosch authorization covering the US Amazon channel or cannot. A secondary, narrower risk applies to Lenovo if any units are refurbished or intended for Amazon Renewed listing, where Lenovo's documented restrictions on non-authorized used dealers could create listing exposure. The Documentation Review dimension was not assessed in this evaluation — no transaction paperwork, invoices, or supply chain documents were available for review, and that limitation applies to both brands.

Supplier LegitimacyVerified

TD SYNNEX is a large, publicly traded (NYSE: SNX), investment-grade-rated IT distributor with decades of operating history (incorporated 2003 as SYNNEX Corp). Evidence across government registrations, SEC filings, BBB profiles, LinkedIn, Fitch ratings, and its own website consistently confirms it as a real, legitimate, large-scale operating business. The domain tdsynnex.com dates to the 2021 merger rebrand and is now ~5.3 years old. The only negative signals are a low-volume Trustpilot score (weak signal given company scale) and third-party recruitment fraud impersonation — the latter concerns scammers posing AS TD SYNNEX, not TD SYNNEX itself. No evidence of registration fabrication, address fraud, website fraud, or vendor-perpetrated scams was found. Contact information (phone numbers, addresses) is consistent and verifiable across multiple independent sources.

Limits of this reading

Identity scope: Supplier legitimacy and identity verification are assessed separately (see the Supplier Identity findings); this analysis addresses the brand/supply-chain relationship only.

Authorization scope: This finding assesses the contractual and commercial authorization relationship between the vendor and the brand — whether the vendor is authorized, recognized, or connected as a source for the brand. It does not assess whether any specific online marketplace will approve resale.

Marketplace eligibility: A confirmed distributor or authorization relationship does not guarantee marketplace approval. Platforms such as Amazon, Walmart, and eBay apply seller-history, category, regional, and brand-specific review that cannot be verified by this analysis. Additional verification with the platform and brand may be required before purchasing inventory.

CONFIRMED POSITIVES

For Lenovo — Lenovo operates a publicly promoted, structured reseller program and is recognized as actively targeting resellers in the B2B channel, confirming an affirmative reseller-friendly posture in its core channel. For Bosch — Bosch publishes a MAP policy that explicitly preserves retailer pricing freedom, and operates broad B2C distribution channels including third-party distributors, indicating resellers are part of Bosch's go-to-market in multiple product lines.

RISK SIGNALS FOUND — REQUIRING VERIFICATION
  • For Lenovo — Seller forum posts allege Lenovo has an agreement with Amazon restricting used/renewed product resale to authorized dealers only, and some Renewed listings have reportedly been blocked.
  • It is unverified whether this restriction applies to new product listings or only to used/renewed channels.
  • Lenovo's DTC expansion is a directional signal worth monitoring but is not a confirmed reseller restriction.
  • These signals require verification against Lenovo's current published channel policy and any Amazon Brand Registry or gating documentation from Lenovo directly.
  • For Bosch — Bosch's sales policy reserves the right to limit distribution channels, and seller forum posts document a Bosch 'whitelist' of authorized sellers and regional restrictions blocking non-EU/UK sellers.
  • A Reddit/ToolGuyd incident from September 2021 documents Bosch threatening legal action against an unauthorized reseller with a statement that Bosch 'has and will continue to file lawsuits against unauthorized resellers' — this event is outside the 24-month recency window and cannot be treated as a current confirmed enforcement action, but the stated ongoing intent warrants verification of whether more recent enforcement actions exist.
  • The Amazon regional restriction messages may reflect Amazon-platform policy rather than Bosch-directed gating, and this distinction requires clarification.
WHAT THE UNKNOWNS DO NOT IMPLY
  • The absence of documented recent enforcement actions against resellers for Lenovo does not establish that resale is without risk — it reflects the limits of the evidence pack.
  • Risk signals found for Bosch do not generalize to Lenovo, and vice versa; each brand's posture is assessed independently.
  • The Bosch divestiture of its Building Technologies division creates uncertainty about channel policy for those specific product lines but does not characterize Bosch's posture across all product categories.

03What we could not confirm

The reading, and its limits

Verdict: Verify Before Purchase. TD SYNNEX holds the strongest possible verified Lenovo distributor credential — US and UK&I Distributor of the Year 2025 and Global Distributor of the Year 2024, supported by a confirmed expanded distribution agreement. For new Lenovo products, the supply chain relationship is well-established. A narrower conditional gap exists for Lenovo: seller forum posts and Amazon Renewed blocking reports indicate Lenovo restricts used and renewed product sales on Amazon to authorized used dealers; whether this applies depends on the condition and category of the specific Lenovo units in this transaction, which has not been confirmed. For Bosch, the leading reading is that TD SYNNEX's relationship is real but geographically scoped to Europe and has not been confirmed for US Amazon resale. The Belgium vendor listing is the only supply-chain link, and it is inferred. Every US-facing Bosch locator reviewed omits TD SYNNEX. Bosch's published policy reserves channel restriction authority, and inferred evidence documents active enforcement against non-authorized Amazon resellers including threatened litigation and listing suspension. Absence from US locators is not itself evidence of wrongdoing or unauthorized sourcing, but it means no positive confirmation of US Amazon resale authorization exists in the record. This reading rests on Bosch authorization for US Amazon resale via TD SYNNEX holding.

04Verification checklist

No.Question to put to your supplier

03Are the Bosch products in this transaction new, refurbished, or used, and under what condition classification would they be listed on Amazon?
04For the Lenovo products in this transaction, are any units intended for listing on Amazon Renewed or under a used/refurbished condition, and if so, can you confirm TD SYNNEX holds authorization for that specific listing category?
05Can you provide a current Lenovo-issued distributor agreement or authorization letter confirming the scope of products and channels covered by TD SYNNEX's Lenovo distribution rights?
06Which specific Bosch division(s) or product line(s) does TD SYNNEX distribute (e.g., Power Tools, Home Appliances, HVAC, Semiconductors, eBike)?
07Can TD SYNNEX provide a manufacturer-issued distribution agreement, authorization letter, or partner portal confirmation from the relevant Bosch division confirming their authorized distributor status?
08In which geographic markets does TD SYNNEX hold its Bosch distribution authorization (e.g., Belgium only, broader EU, US, global)?
09Is TD SYNNEX listed on the relevant Bosch division's official authorized distributor or dealer locator (e.g., Bosch Professional Tools dealer search, Bosch Semiconductors distributor list)?
10Does TD SYNNEX have a dedicated Bosch vendor page on its US or global portal (similar to its Lenovo pages at tdsynnex.com/na/us/lenovo/), and if so, does it carry an 'Authorized Distributor' designation from Bosch?
11Does Lenovo publish a current channel or reseller policy that specifies any restrictions on third-party Amazon sales of new (non-used) products?
12Has Lenovo issued any documented enforcement actions (C&Ds, IP complaints, or Amazon takedown requests) against third-party resellers of new Lenovo products within the last 24 months?
13Is Lenovo's DTC expansion (as reported by Modern Retail) accompanied by any formal policy changes restricting third-party reseller access to Lenovo products?
14Does Bosch publish a current, brand-side policy document confirming that only authorized sellers may list Bosch products on Amazon, and if so, which product categories does it cover?
15Has Bosch issued any documented C&Ds or filed lawsuits against third-party resellers (not counterfeiters) within the last 24 months?
16Are the Amazon regional restrictions on Bosch products (blocking non-EU/UK sellers) driven by a Bosch-directed gating policy or by Amazon's own platform compliance rules?
17Following Bosch's divestiture of its Building Technologies security and communications division to Triton, what entity now controls channel and reseller policy for those product lines, and has that policy changed?
