## WHY NOW (one paragraph)

Track 3 answers the question the other tracks can't: does the BRAND make third-party resale risky, independent of how legitimate the supplier is? It is the highest-weighted dimension (0.30), it is why every case today is capped at Usable With Conditions — **no supplier can reach Source Clear until this ships** — and its entry conditions have been enforced by a failing test since H7 (`firewallRegistry.test.ts`: registry entries + ADR-T1-001 collision audit, or the build cannot proceed). The identity layer beneath it is now hardened end-to-end (SB-1/SB-2), and the pre-Track-3 sweep returned READY with no HIGH defect.

**AMENDMENT 2 (founder-ruled): cross-track collision audit vs Track 1/2 hard-fail keys — PERFORMED, results:**

Track 1 vetoes (`registration_fabricated`, `address_fraudulent`, `website_fraudulent`, `scam_reports_corroborated`) and Track 2's (`counterfeit_channel`, `conflicting_authorization`) were checked against all four Track 3 vetoes. **No mislabeling collision exists** — every Track 1/2 veto is about the VENDOR (its registration, its site, its conduct, its claimed relationship); every Track 3 veto is about the BRAND's posture. Mechanical backstop already frozen: firewall gate ④ rejects any key proposed by the wrong track (`weightKeyExistsInAnyTrack` distinguishes wrong-track from unknown). **Two legitimate DUAL-FACET events documented** (one real-world fact lawfully informing two dimensions — permitted by design, because dimensions are lenses):

| one underlying event | Track 2 may propose | Track 3 may propose | why this is NOT double-counting |
|---|---|---|---|
| The brand takes IP action against THIS vendor | `counterfeit_channel` (this vendor's channel carries suspect goods) | `active_ip_complaints` (the brand enforces against resellers) | Vetoes do not stack: `hard_fail` locks per-track and the verdict lock is idempotent — a second veto changes nothing numerically. Each track's finding names its own subject; narrative reconciliation across tracks is the Synthesis Engine's job (OQ-F precedent), not Track 3's. |
| The brand issues a C&D naming THIS vendor | `conflicting_authorization` (the claimed relationship is contradicted) | `cease_and_desist_distributed` (the brand C&Ds resellers) | Same: per-track subjects, idempotent verdict lock, no score interaction across tracks (weights are per-track). |

**Prompt obligation from Amendment 2:** each track's prompt states its SUBJECT explicitly (Track 3: "the brand's posture toward resellers generally — an action against the investigated vendor specifically is ALSO Track 2's subject; report the brand-posture facet only, with the citation") so dual-facet events are reported as two facets, never one fact drifting into the wrong dimension.

Absence-of-evidence rule holds everywhere: none of the four may ever be proposed from "we found nothing" (the Zzqxwv attempt-1 lesson, now prompt law).
