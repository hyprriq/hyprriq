The single most important risk

A direct authorization link between TD Synnex and the brands in scope could not be independently confirmed. TD Synnex is a verifiable, large-scale technology distributor with a clear operating presence, and it is credible that it carries these brands through established distribution channels. However, the research could not locate independent confirmation — from the brands’ own reseller directories or from documentation you supplied — that this specific distributor is an authorized channel for Bosch and Lenovo goods sold onward to your account. This does not indicate the supplier is illegitimate; it means the authorization chain the marketplace and the brand may later ask you to evidence is, at this point, unconfirmed. Treat a first order as a test, and confirm the items below before committing significant capital.

Verify before you commit

The five that matter most, in priority order.

  1. Ask the distributor to confirm, in writing, the channel through which the brands in scope are supplied to your account.
  2. Request a recent invoice or order confirmation showing the brands in scope from this distributor.
  3. Confirm whether the distributor’s terms permit onward resale on your intended marketplace.
  4. Check each brand’s official reseller directory for this distributor before a large order.
  5. Place a small test order and verify the goods and paperwork on arrival before scaling.
