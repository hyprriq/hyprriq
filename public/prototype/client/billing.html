How credits work

What does one credit cover?
One case: one supplier and up to 5 brands, researched together and delivered as a single report.
Do unused plan credits roll over?
Your balance and your plan allotment are separate things: the balance shows what you can spend right now; the plan adds 5 each cycle. Details of rollover limits live in your plan terms.
What if a supplier can’t be researched?
If we can’t start research on a case, the credit returns to your balance automatically.

Subscription

Cancelling stops future renewals. Your completed reports stay available to read, and remaining credits stay usable until the end of the current cycle.
