Research & reports

What does a report actually tell me?
One clear verdict on a four-level scale, the single most important risk in plain language, findings across five assessment areas, an honest split between what we confirmed and what we couldn’t, and a prioritized checklist of what to verify with the supplier before you commit.
Does a “Source Clear” verdict mean the supplier is guaranteed?
No — no report can promise that. A verdict reflects what the observable evidence supported at the time of research. Source Clear means we found consistency and no significant gaps; the decision, and standard diligence, stay with you.
How long does research take?
Within 5 business days on your plan. If we need something from you — like confirming the brand scope — the clock pauses until you answer, and resumes right after.
What if you can’t find enough on a supplier?
The report says so, honestly — what we looked for and couldn’t confirm is part of the deliverable, not a failure of it. If we can’t research a case at all, the credit returns to your balance.

Credits & billing

What does one credit cover?
One case: one supplier and up to 5 brands, researched together and delivered as one report.
When do my credits renew?
Each billing cycle your plan adds 5 credits. Your current balance always shows on the Billing page — it and the plan allotment are separate numbers.
What happens to my reports if I cancel?
Completed reports stay available to read. Cancelling stops future renewals; submitting new research needs an active plan.
